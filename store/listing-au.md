**Full description**

Get ready for the Australian citizenship test with Oathly.

Oathly's practice questions are written from the official resource book, "Australian Citizenship: Our Common Bond", covering all four testable parts:
• Australia and its people
• Australia's democratic beliefs, rights and liberties
• Government and the law in Australia
• Australian values

PRACTISE LIKE THE REAL TEST
• Practice tests with 20 multiple-choice questions, including 5 on Australian values
• The real pass rule: 75% overall AND every values question correct
• Clear explanations after each answer
• Hear questions read aloud

LEARN FASTER
• Study cards by part, or all questions
• Spaced repetition brings back the questions you miss
• "Chance of passing today" estimate, based on your own answers
• Daily reminder and streaks
• Works offline. No account needed. Your progress stays on your phone.
