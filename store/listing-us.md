**Full description**

Get ready for the civics test at your U.S. naturalization interview with Oathly.

Oathly uses the civics questions and answers published by USCIS, for both versions of the test:
• 2025 civics test: 128 questions. The officer asks up to 20 and you need 12 right. For Form N-400 filed on or after October 20, 2025.
• 2008 civics test: 100 questions. The officer asks up to 10 and you need 6 right. For applications filed before October 20, 2025.
• 65/20 mode: if you are 65 or older and have been a permanent resident for 20 years, study only the 20 starred questions.

PRACTISE LIKE THE REAL INTERVIEW
• Mock interview with the real stopping rules: it ends as soon as you pass or fail, just like with the officer.
• Hear every question read aloud, then say your answer out loud or type it.
• Smart answer checking that understands "the Constitution", "U.S. Constitution" and "27 or twenty-seven".

ANSWERS THAT STAY CURRENT
Some answers change after elections and appointments, or depend on where you live. Choose your state and Oathly shows your own senators, representative, governor and capital, plus the current President, Vice President, Speaker and Chief Justice. The answers are checked against public data every day, and you get a notification when one of yours changes.

LEARN FASTER
• Study cards for all questions or by topic
• Spaced repetition brings back the questions you miss
• "Chance of passing today" estimate, based on your own answers
• Daily reminder and streaks
• Works offline. No account needed. Your progress stays on your phone.

Free with ads. One optional purchase removes ads forever; every study feature stays free.
