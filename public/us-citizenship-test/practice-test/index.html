Free U.S. citizenship practice test

This practice interview works like the real civics test. You get random questions, you say each answer out loud, then you check it. The test stops as soon as you pass or can no longer pass, just as it does with the officer.

Used to show your own senators, governor and capital. It stays in your browser.

How the practice interview is scored

  • 2025 civics test: up to 20 questions. You pass with 12 correct answers; the test ends early if you miss 9.
  • 2008 civics test: up to 10 questions. You pass with 6 correct answers; the test ends early if you miss 5.
  • 65/20: 10 questions from the 20 marked ones. You pass with 6 correct answers.

Be honest when you mark yourself. At the interview the officer accepts any answer from the official list, even if your wording is a little different, but not an answer that is only close.

Want your answers checked for you?

In the Oathly app you speak or type your answer and the app checks it, with questions read aloud, your state answers built in, and a daily estimate of your chance of passing.
