Texas citizenship test answers

If you live in Texas, these are your answers to the civics test questions that depend on your state. They are checked against public records every day and were last updated on November 12, 2026.

U.S. senators for Texas

question 23 (2025 test) · question 20 (2008 test)

Who is one of your state’s U.S. senators now?

Answer for Texas

  • John Cornyn
  • Ted Cruz

Either senator is a correct answer. You only need to name one.
