If you live in Florida, these are your answers to the civics test questions that depend on your state. They are checked against public records every day and were last updated on November 2, 2026.

U.S. senators for Florida

question 23 (2025 test) · question 20 (2008 test)

Who is one of your state’s U.S. senators now?

Answer for Florida

  • Ashley Moody
  • Rick Scott

Either senator is a correct answer. You only need to name one.

U.S. representatives for Florida

question 29 (2025 test) · question 23 (2008 test)

Name your U.S. representative.

Florida has 28 congressional districts, and the correct answer is the representative for the district where you live. If you do not know your district, enter your ZIP code at house.gov.
