Why did the United States enter the Persian Gulf War?

2025 civics test · question 114 of 128American History: Recent American History and Other Important Historical Information

Answer

  • To force the Iraqi military from Kuwait

Why this is the answer

In August 1990, Iraq invaded its small neighbor Kuwait. In early 1991, the U.S. led a group of many countries that drove the Iraqi army out of Kuwait.
