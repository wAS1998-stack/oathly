What territory did the United States buy from France in 1803?

2025 civics test · question 90 of 128American History: 1800s

Answer (any one of these)

  • Louisiana Territory
  • Louisiana

You only need to give one of these answers. Pick the one that is easiest for you to remember and say.

Why this is the answer

In 1803, under President Jefferson, the U.S. paid France about $15 million for the Louisiana Territory, west of the Mississippi River. It doubled the size of the country.
