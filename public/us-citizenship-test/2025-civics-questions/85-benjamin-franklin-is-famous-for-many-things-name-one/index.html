Benjamin Franklin is famous for many things. Name one.

2025 civics test · question 85 of 128American History: Colonial Period and Independence

Answer (any one of these)

  • Founded the first free public libraries
  • First Postmaster General of the United States
  • Helped write the Declaration of Independence
  • Inventor
  • U.S. diplomat

You only need to give one of these answers. Pick the one that is easiest for you to remember and say.

Why this is the answer

Franklin's inventions include the lightning rod and the Franklin stove. As a U.S. diplomat in France, he helped win French support during the Revolutionary War.
