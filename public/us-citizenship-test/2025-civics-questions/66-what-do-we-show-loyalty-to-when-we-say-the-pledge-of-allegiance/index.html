What do we show loyalty to when we say the Pledge of Allegiance?

2025 civics test · question 66 of 128American Government: Rights and Responsibilities65/20 question

Answer (any one of these)

  • The United States
  • The flag

You only need to give one of these answers. Pick the one that is easiest for you to remember and say.

Why this is the answer

The Pledge begins, 'I pledge allegiance to the Flag of the United States of America, and to the Republic for which it stands.' Allegiance means loyalty.
