What is the name of the Speaker of the House of Representatives now?

2025 civics test · question 30 of 128American Government: System of Government65/20 question

Answer

  • Mike Johnson

This answer changes after elections and appointments. Oathly checks it against public records every day; it was last updated on November 12, 2026. You can confirm it on the USCIS test updates page.

Why this is the answer

The Speaker leads the House of Representatives and is chosen by its members. The Speaker is second in line to become president, after the vice president.
