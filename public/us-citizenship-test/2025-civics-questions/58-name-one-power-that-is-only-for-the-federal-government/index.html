Name one power that is only for the federal government.

2025 civics test · question 58 of 128American Government: System of Government

Answer (any one of these)

  • Print paper money
  • Mint coins
  • Declare war
  • Create an army
  • Make treaties
  • Set foreign policy

You only need to give one of these answers. Pick the one that is easiest for you to remember and say.

Why this is the answer

These powers belong to the national government so the country acts as one nation, with one kind of money and one voice with other countries. States cannot print money or make treaties.
