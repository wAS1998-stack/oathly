What does the judicial branch do?

2025 civics test · question 51 of 128American Government: System of Government

Answer (any one of these)

  • Reviews laws
  • Explains laws
  • Resolves disputes (disagreements) about the law
  • Decides if a law goes against the (U.S.) Constitution

You only need to give one of these answers. Pick the one that is easiest for you to remember and say.

Why this is the answer

Courts decide what laws mean and settle disagreements about them. The power to decide that a law goes against the Constitution is called judicial review.
