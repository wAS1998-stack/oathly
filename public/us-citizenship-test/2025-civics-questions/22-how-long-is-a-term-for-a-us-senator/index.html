How long is a term for a U.S. senator?

2025 civics test · question 22 of 128American Government: System of Government

Answer

  • Six (6) years

Why this is the answer

Senators serve six-year terms. Their elections are spread out, so only about one-third of Senate seats are up for election every two years.
