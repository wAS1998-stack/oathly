What are three rights of everyone living in the United States?

2025 civics test · question 65 of 128American Government: Rights and Responsibilities

Answer (name 3)

  • Freedom of expression
  • Freedom of speech
  • Freedom of assembly
  • Freedom to petition the government
  • Freedom of religion
  • The right to bear arms

The officer needs 3 answers for this question. Any 3 from the list are fine.

Why this is the answer

These rights come from the First and Second Amendments in the Bill of Rights. They protect everyone living in the United States, not only citizens.
