Who lived in America before the Europeans arrived?

2025 civics test · question 74 of 128American History: Colonial Period and Independence65/20 question

Answer (any one of these)

  • American Indians
  • Native Americans

You only need to give one of these answers. Pick the one that is easiest for you to remember and say.

Why this is the answer

Native Americans, also called American Indians, lived across North America for thousands of years before Europeans arrived. They formed many nations with different languages and cultures.
