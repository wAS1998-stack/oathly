The executive branch has many parts. Name one.

2025 civics test · question 46 of 128American Government: System of Government

Answer (any one of these)

  • President (of the United States)
  • Cabinet
  • Federal departments and agencies

You only need to give one of these answers. Pick the one that is easiest for you to remember and say.

Why this is the answer

The executive branch carries out the laws and includes the president, the Cabinet, and many departments and agencies. USCIS, which handles citizenship, is part of the Department of Homeland Security.
