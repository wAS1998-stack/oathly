Name one power of the president.

2025 civics test · question 41 of 128American Government: System of Government

Answer (any one of these)

  • Signs bills into law
  • Vetoes bills
  • Enforces laws
  • Commander in Chief (of the military)
  • Chief diplomat
  • Appoints federal judges

You only need to give one of these answers. Pick the one that is easiest for you to remember and say.

Why this is the answer

Some of the president's powers are checks on the other branches. The president can veto bills passed by Congress and chooses federal judges, who must then be approved by the Senate.
