There are three branches of government. Why?

2025 civics test · question 15 of 128American Government: Principles of American Government

Answer (any one of these)

  • So one part does not become too powerful
  • Checks and balances
  • Separation of powers

You only need to give one of these answers. Pick the one that is easiest for you to remember and say.

Why this is the answer

Splitting power among Congress, the president, and the courts lets each branch check the others. This keeps any one person or group from taking too much power.
