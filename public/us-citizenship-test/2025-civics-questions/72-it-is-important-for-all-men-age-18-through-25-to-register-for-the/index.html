It is important for all men age 18 through 25 to register for the Selective Service. Name one reason why.

2025 civics test · question 72 of 128American Government: Rights and Responsibilities

Answer (any one of these)

  • Required by law
  • Civic duty
  • Makes the draft fair, if needed

You only need to give one of these answers. Pick the one that is easiest for you to remember and say.

Why this is the answer

Selective Service keeps a list of men who could be called to serve if a military draft is ever needed. Most immigrant men ages 18 through 25 living in the U.S. must register too.
