How are changes made to the U.S. Constitution?

2025 civics test · question 5 of 128American Government: Principles of American Government

Answer (any one of these)

  • Amendments
  • The amendment process

You only need to give one of these answers. Pick the one that is easiest for you to remember and say.

Why this is the answer

An amendment is a change or addition to the Constitution. Usually, two-thirds of both the House and the Senate must vote for it, and then three-fourths of the states must approve it.
