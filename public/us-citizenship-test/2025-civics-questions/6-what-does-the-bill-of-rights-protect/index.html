What does the Bill of Rights protect?

2025 civics test · question 6 of 128American Government: Principles of American Government

Answer (any one of these)

  • (The basic) rights of Americans
  • (The basic) rights of people living in the United States

You only need to give one of these answers. Pick the one that is easiest for you to remember and say.

Why this is the answer

The Bill of Rights is the first ten amendments, added in 1791. It protects basic freedoms like speech and religion, and the right to a fair trial.
