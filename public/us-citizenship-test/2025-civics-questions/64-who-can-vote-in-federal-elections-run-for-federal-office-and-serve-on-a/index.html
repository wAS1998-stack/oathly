Who can vote in federal elections, run for federal office, and serve on a jury in the United States?

2025 civics test · question 64 of 128American Government: Rights and Responsibilities

Answer (any one of these)

  • Citizens
  • Citizens of the United States
  • U.S. citizens

You only need to give one of these answers. Pick the one that is easiest for you to remember and say.

Why this is the answer

Permanent residents (green card holders) share many freedoms with citizens. But voting in federal elections, running for federal office, and serving on a jury are for citizens only.
