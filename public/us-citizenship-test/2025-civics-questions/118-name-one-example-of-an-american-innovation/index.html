Name one example of an American innovation.

2025 civics test · question 118 of 128American History: Recent American History and Other Important Historical Information

Answer (any one of these)

  • Light bulb
  • Automobile (cars, internal combustion engine)
  • Skyscrapers
  • Airplane
  • Assembly line
  • Landing on the moon
  • Integrated circuit (IC)

You only need to give one of these answers. Pick the one that is easiest for you to remember and say.

Why this is the answer

The Wright brothers made the first successful powered airplane flight in 1903, and Henry Ford's assembly line made cars cheaper. In 1969, American astronauts became the first people to walk on the moon.
