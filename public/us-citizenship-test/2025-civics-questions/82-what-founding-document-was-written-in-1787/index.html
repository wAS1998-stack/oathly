What founding document was written in 1787?

2025 civics test · question 82 of 128American History: Colonial Period and Independence

Answer

  • (U.S.) Constitution

Why this is the answer

Delegates met in Philadelphia from May to September 1787 and signed the Constitution on September 17. That date is now Constitution Day and Citizenship Day.
