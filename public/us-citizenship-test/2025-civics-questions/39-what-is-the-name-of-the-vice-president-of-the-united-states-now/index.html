What is the name of the Vice President of the United States now?

2025 civics test · question 39 of 128American Government: System of Government65/20 question

Answer

  • J.D. Vance

This answer changes after elections and appointments. Oathly checks it against public records every day; it was last updated on November 12, 2026. You can confirm it on the USCIS test updates page.

Why this is the answer

The vice president is elected with the president and becomes president if the president can no longer serve. The vice president also serves as President of the Senate and can break tie votes there.
