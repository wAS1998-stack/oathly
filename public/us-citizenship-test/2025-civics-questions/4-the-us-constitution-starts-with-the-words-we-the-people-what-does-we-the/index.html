The U.S. Constitution starts with the words “We the People.” What does “We the People” mean?

2025 civics test · question 4 of 128American Government: Principles of American Government

Answer (any one of these)

  • Self-government
  • Popular sovereignty
  • Consent of the governed
  • People should govern themselves
  • (Example of) social contract

You only need to give one of these answers. Pick the one that is easiest for you to remember and say.

Why this is the answer

These first words show that the government's power comes from the people. Citizens choose their leaders and agree to be governed, which is the idea of self-government.
