When did all men get the right to vote?

2025 civics test · question 98 of 128American History: 1800s

Answer (any one of these)

  • After the Civil War
  • During Reconstruction
  • (With the) 15th Amendment
  • 1870

You only need to give one of these answers. Pick the one that is easiest for you to remember and say.

Why this is the answer

The 15th Amendment was added in 1870, during Reconstruction, the period after the Civil War. It says citizens cannot be denied the vote because of race or color, or because they were once enslaved.
