The colonists came to America for many reasons. Name one.

2025 civics test · question 73 of 128American History: Colonial Period and Independence

Answer (any one of these)

  • Freedom
  • Political liberty
  • Religious freedom
  • Economic opportunity
  • Escape persecution

You only need to give one of these answers. Pick the one that is easiest for you to remember and say.

Why this is the answer

Many early colonists, like the Pilgrims who came on the Mayflower in 1620, wanted religious freedom. Others came for economic opportunity, such as the chance to own land.
