The Nation’s first motto was “E Pluribus Unum.” What does that mean?

2025 civics test · question 124 of 128Symbols and Holidays: Symbols

Answer (any one of these)

  • Out of many, one
  • We all become one

You only need to give one of these answers. Pick the one that is easiest for you to remember and say.

Why this is the answer

This Latin motto describes the 13 colonies uniting as one nation, and it is on the Great Seal and U.S. coins. 'In God We Trust' became the official national motto in 1956.
