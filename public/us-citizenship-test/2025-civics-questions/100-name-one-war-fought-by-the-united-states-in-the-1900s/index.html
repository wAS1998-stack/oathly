Name one war fought by the United States in the 1900s.

2025 civics test · question 100 of 128American History: Recent American History and Other Important Historical Information

Answer (any one of these)

  • World War I
  • World War II
  • Korean War
  • Vietnam War
  • (Persian) Gulf War

You only need to give one of these answers. Pick the one that is easiest for you to remember and say.

Why this is the answer

The U.S. fought in both world wars, then in Korea and Vietnam during the Cold War. The Persian Gulf War, in 1991, forced Iraq's army out of Kuwait.
