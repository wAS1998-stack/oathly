Some states have more representatives than other states. Why?

2025 civics test · question 35 of 128American Government: System of Government

Answer (any one of these)

  • (Because of) the state’s population
  • (Because) they have more people
  • (Because) some states have more people

You only need to give one of these answers. Pick the one that is easiest for you to remember and say.

Why this is the answer

The census counts the population every 10 years. After each count, the 435 House seats are divided again, so a state that grows faster than others can gain seats.
