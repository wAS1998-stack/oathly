How many amendments does the U.S. Constitution have?

2025 civics test · question 7 of 128American Government: Principles of American Government65/20 question

Answer

  • Twenty-seven (27)

Why this is the answer

Changing the Constitution is hard, so it has been amended only 27 times since it was written in 1787. The first ten amendments, the Bill of Rights, were added together in 1791.
