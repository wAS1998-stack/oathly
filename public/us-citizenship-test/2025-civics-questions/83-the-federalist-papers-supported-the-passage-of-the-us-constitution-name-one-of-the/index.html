The Federalist Papers supported the passage of the U.S. Constitution. Name one of the writers.

2025 civics test · question 83 of 128American History: Colonial Period and Independence

Answer (any one of these)

  • (James) Madison
  • (Alexander) Hamilton
  • (John) Jay
  • Publius

You only need to give one of these answers. Pick the one that is easiest for you to remember and say.

Why this is the answer

Madison, Hamilton, and Jay wrote 85 essays in 1787 and 1788 to convince New Yorkers to support the new Constitution. They signed them with one shared pen name, 'Publius.'
