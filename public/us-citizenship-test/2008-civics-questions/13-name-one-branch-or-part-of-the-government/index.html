Name one branch or part of the government.

2008 civics test · question 13 of 100American Government: System of Government65/20 question

Answer (any one of these)

  • Congress
  • legislative
  • President
  • executive
  • the courts
  • judicial

You only need to give one of these answers. Pick the one that is easiest for you to remember and say.

Why this is the answer

There are three branches. Congress (legislative) makes laws, the President (executive) carries them out, and the courts (judicial) explain them.
