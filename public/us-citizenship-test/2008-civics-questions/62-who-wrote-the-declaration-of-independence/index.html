Who wrote the Declaration of Independence?

2008 civics test · question 62 of 100American History: Colonial Period and Independence

Answer

  • (Thomas) Jefferson

Why this is the answer

Thomas Jefferson of Virginia wrote the first draft in 1776, and the Continental Congress adopted it on July 4. Jefferson later became the third President.
