Who was President during the Great Depression and World War II?

2008 civics test · question 80 of 100American History: Recent American History and Other Important Historical Information

Answer

  • (Franklin) Roosevelt

Why this is the answer

Franklin D. Roosevelt (FDR) was elected four times, more than any other president, and served from 1933 until his death in 1945. His New Deal programs were created to fight the Depression.
