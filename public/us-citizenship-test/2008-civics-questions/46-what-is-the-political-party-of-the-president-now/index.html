What is the political party of the President now?

2008 civics test · question 46 of 100American Government: System of Government

Answer

  • Republican (Party)

This answer changes after elections and appointments. Oathly checks it against public records every day; it was last updated on November 2, 2026. You can confirm it on the USCIS test updates page.

Why this is the answer

Which party holds the presidency can change after each presidential election, held every four years. In modern times, every president has been either a Democrat or a Republican.
