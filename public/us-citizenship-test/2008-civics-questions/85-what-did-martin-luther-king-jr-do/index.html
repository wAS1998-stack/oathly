What did Martin Luther King, Jr. do?

2008 civics test · question 85 of 100American History: Recent American History and Other Important Historical Information65/20 question

Answer (any one of these)

  • fought for civil rights
  • worked for equality for all Americans

You only need to give one of these answers. Pick the one that is easiest for you to remember and say.

Why this is the answer

King, a minister, led peaceful protests against racial discrimination. At the 1963 March on Washington, he gave his famous 'I Have a Dream' speech.
