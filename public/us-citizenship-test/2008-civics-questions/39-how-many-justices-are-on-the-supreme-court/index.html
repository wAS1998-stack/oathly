How many justices are on the Supreme Court?

2008 civics test · question 39 of 100American Government: System of Government

Answer

  • Nine (9)

This answer changes after elections and appointments. Oathly checks it against public records every day; it was last updated on November 2, 2026. You can confirm it on the USCIS test updates page.

Why this is the answer

The Constitution does not say how many justices there are, so Congress sets the number by law. The Court is led by the Chief Justice, and the others are called Associate Justices.
