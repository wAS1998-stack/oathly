Name one U.S. territory.

2008 civics test · question 91 of 100Integrated Civics: Geography

Answer (any one of these)

  • Puerto Rico
  • U.S. Virgin Islands
  • American Samoa
  • Northern Mariana Islands
  • Guam

You only need to give one of these answers. Pick the one that is easiest for you to remember and say.

Why this is the answer

Territories belong to the United States but are not states. Puerto Rico and the U.S. Virgin Islands are in the Caribbean, and Guam, American Samoa, and the Northern Mariana Islands are in the Pacific.
