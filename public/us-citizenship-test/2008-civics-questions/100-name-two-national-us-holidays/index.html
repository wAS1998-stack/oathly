Name two national U.S. holidays.

2008 civics test · question 100 of 100Integrated Civics: Holidays

Answer (name 2)

  • New Year’s Day
  • Martin Luther King, Jr. Day
  • Presidents’ Day
  • Memorial Day
  • Independence Day
  • Labor Day
  • Columbus Day
  • Veterans Day
  • Thanksgiving
  • Christmas

The officer needs 2 answers for this question. Any 2 from the list are fine.

Why this is the answer

These are federal holidays, when most federal offices close. Some fall on a fixed date, like Independence Day on July 4, while others fall on a set Monday, like Labor Day in September.
