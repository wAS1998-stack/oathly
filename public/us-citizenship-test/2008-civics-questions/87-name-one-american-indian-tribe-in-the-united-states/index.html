Name one American Indian tribe in the United States.

2008 civics test · question 87 of 100American History: Recent American History and Other Important Historical Information

Answer (any one of these)

  • Cherokee
  • Navajo
  • Sioux
  • Chippewa
  • Choctaw
  • Pueblo
  • Apache
  • Iroquois
  • Creek
  • Blackfeet
  • Seminole
  • Cheyenne
  • Arawak
  • Shawnee
  • Mohegan
  • Huron
  • Oneida
  • Lakota
  • Crow
  • Teton
  • Hopi
  • Inuit

You only need to give one of these answers. Pick the one that is easiest for you to remember and say.

Why this is the answer

There are hundreds of American Indian tribes in the United States, each with its own history, culture, and government. The Cherokee and the Navajo are two of the largest.
