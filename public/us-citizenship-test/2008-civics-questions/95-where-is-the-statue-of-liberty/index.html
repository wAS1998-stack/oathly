Where is the Statue of Liberty?

2008 civics test · question 95 of 100Integrated Civics: Geography65/20 question

Answer (any one of these)

  • New York (Harbor)
  • Liberty Island
  • New Jersey
  • near New York City
  • on the Hudson (River)

You only need to give one of these answers. Pick the one that is easiest for you to remember and say.

Why this is the answer

The Statue of Liberty was a gift from the people of France, dedicated in 1886. It stands on Liberty Island in New York Harbor and became a symbol of welcome for immigrants.
