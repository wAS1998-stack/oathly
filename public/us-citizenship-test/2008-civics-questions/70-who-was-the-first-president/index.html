Who was the first President?

2008 civics test · question 70 of 100American History: Colonial Period and Independence65/20 question

Answer

  • (George) Washington

Why this is the answer

George Washington became the first President in 1789 and served two terms. He chose to step down after eight years, which started a two-term tradition.
