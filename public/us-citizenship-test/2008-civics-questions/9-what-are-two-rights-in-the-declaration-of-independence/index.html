What are two rights in the Declaration of Independence?

2008 civics test · question 9 of 100American Government: Principles of American Democracy

Answer (name 2)

  • life
  • liberty
  • pursuit of happiness

The officer needs 2 answers for this question. Any 2 from the list are fine.

Why this is the answer

The Declaration says all people are born with rights that cannot be taken away, including 'Life, Liberty and the pursuit of Happiness.' It says governments exist to protect these rights.
