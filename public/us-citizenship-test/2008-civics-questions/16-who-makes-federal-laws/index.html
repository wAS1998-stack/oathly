Who makes federal laws?

2008 civics test · question 16 of 100American Government: System of Government

Answer (any one of these)

  • Congress
  • Senate and House (of Representatives)
  • (U.S. or national) legislature

You only need to give one of these answers. Pick the one that is easiest for you to remember and say.

Why this is the answer

Congress is the legislative branch, made up of the Senate and the House of Representatives. A bill must pass both before it goes to the President to sign.
