What did Susan B. Anthony do?

2008 civics test · question 77 of 100American History: 1800s

Answer (any one of these)

  • fought for women’s rights
  • fought for civil rights

You only need to give one of these answers. Pick the one that is easiest for you to remember and say.

Why this is the answer

Anthony spent decades working for women's right to vote and was arrested for voting in 1872. The 19th Amendment, which protects women's right to vote, was added in 1920, after her death.
