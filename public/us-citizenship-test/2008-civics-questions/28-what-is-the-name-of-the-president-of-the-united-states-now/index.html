What is the name of the President of the United States now?

2008 civics test · question 28 of 100American Government: System of Government65/20 question

Answer

  • Donald Trump

This answer changes after elections and appointments. Oathly checks it against public records every day; it was last updated on November 2, 2026. You can confirm it on the USCIS test updates page.

Why this is the answer

The President leads the executive branch and is Commander in Chief of the military. Presidents are elected every four years and can be elected no more than twice.
