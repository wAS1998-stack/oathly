What is one right or freedom from the First Amendment?

2008 civics test · question 6 of 100American Government: Principles of American Democracy65/20 question

Answer (any one of these)

  • speech
  • religion
  • assembly
  • press
  • petition the government

You only need to give one of these answers. Pick the one that is easiest for you to remember and say.

Why this is the answer

To remember the five First Amendment freedoms, think RAPPS: Religion, Assembly, Press, Petition, and Speech. The First Amendment is part of the Bill of Rights, added in 1791.
