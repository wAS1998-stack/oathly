Name one right only for United States citizens.

2008 civics test · question 50 of 100American Government: Rights and Responsibilities

Answer (any one of these)

  • vote in a federal election
  • run for federal office

You only need to give one of these answers. Pick the one that is easiest for you to remember and say.

Why this is the answer

Permanent residents share many rights, but only citizens can vote in federal elections. The Constitution also requires the President and members of Congress to be citizens.
