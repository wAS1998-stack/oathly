When was the Declaration of Independence adopted?

2008 civics test · question 63 of 100American History: Colonial Period and Independence

Answer

  • July 4, 1776

Why this is the answer

The Continental Congress adopted the Declaration of Independence on July 4, 1776, in Philadelphia. That is why Americans celebrate Independence Day every July 4.
