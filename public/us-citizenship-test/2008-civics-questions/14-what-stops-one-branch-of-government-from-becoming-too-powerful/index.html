What stops one branch of government from becoming too powerful?

2008 civics test · question 14 of 100American Government: System of Government

Answer (any one of these)

  • checks and balances
  • separation of powers

You only need to give one of these answers. Pick the one that is easiest for you to remember and say.

Why this is the answer

Power is split among three branches, and each can limit the others. For example, the President can veto a bill, but Congress can override the veto with a two-thirds vote in both houses.
