What ocean is on the East Coast of the United States?

2008 civics test · question 90 of 100Integrated Civics: Geography

Answer

  • Atlantic (Ocean)

Why this is the answer

The East Coast runs along the Atlantic Ocean from Maine to Florida. All 13 original colonies were in this eastern part of the country.
