Who is the Chief Justice of the United States now?

2008 civics test · question 40 of 100American Government: System of Government

Answer

  • John G. Roberts, Jr.

This answer changes after elections and appointments. Oathly checks it against public records every day; it was last updated on November 2, 2026. You can confirm it on the USCIS test updates page.

Why this is the answer

The Chief Justice leads the Supreme Court and the federal court system. Like all justices, the Chief Justice is appointed by the President, approved by the Senate, and can serve for life.
