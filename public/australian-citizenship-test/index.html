Common questions

How many questions are on the Australian citizenship test?

The test has 20 multiple-choice questions. 5 of them are about Australian values. You have 45 minutes.

What is the pass mark for the Australian citizenship test?

You need at least 75% overall, which is 15 of 20, and you must answer all 5 Australian values questions correctly. One wrong values question means you do not pass, even with a high total score.

What do I need to study?

Everything in the test comes from the testable section of the official booklet, Australian Citizenship: Our Common Bond. It has four parts: Australia and its people; Australia's democratic beliefs, rights and liberties; Government and the law in Australia; and Australian values.

Is the test in English?

Yes. The test is a computer-based, multiple-choice test conducted in English.

Are these the real test questions?

No. The real questions are not published. Oathly's practice questions are original and are written from the testable section of Our Common Bond, so they cover the same facts in the same multiple-choice format.
