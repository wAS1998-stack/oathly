Oathly Privacy Policy

Effective 1 November 2026

Oathly is an independent app that helps people practise for the U.S. and Australian citizenship tests. It is not affiliated with USCIS, the Australian Department of Home Affairs, or any government agency. This policy explains what information the app handles and why.

The short version

  • No account, no sign-up. We never ask for your name, email, phone number or address.
  • Your study progress and settings stay on your phone.
  • The free version shows ads from Google AdMob, which uses device information as described below.

Information stored on your device

Oathly saves your answers, study progress, test results and settings (for example the test you chose and, for the U.S. test, the state and representative you picked so the app can show the right answers). This information stays on your device. If Android backup is turned on, Android may include it in your own device backup. You can delete it at any time with Settings → Reset progress or by uninstalling the app.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The free version of Oathly shows ads provided by Google AdMob. To show, measure and protect ads, Google may collect your device's advertising ID, IP address, device and app information, and how you interact with ads. Where the law requires it (for example in the UK, the European Economic Area and some U.S. states), Oathly asks for your consent with Google's consent form before personalised ads are shown, and you can change your choice later in Settings → Privacy choices. Learn more in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's settings. Users who buy Oathly Pro see no ads.

Purchases

The optional Oathly Pro purchase is handled entirely by Google Play. Oathly receives only a confirmation that the purchase is valid; we never see your payment details.

Speech features

When you tap Say it, your phone's speech recognition service (for example Google's) turns your voice into text and gives Oathly the text. Oathly uses that text only to check your answer on your device; it does not record, store or send your voice. Questions are read aloud with your phone's built-in text-to-speech, which works offline.

Answer updates

Once a day the app downloads a small file of public information (current officeholders and members of Congress) from oathly.pages.dev, hosted by Cloudflare. This request contains no personal information. Like any web server, Cloudflare may briefly log technical data such as IP addresses to operate and secure the service.

Children

Oathly is made for adults preparing for citizenship and is not directed to children under 13.

Security

All network connections use HTTPS.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and its effective date.
